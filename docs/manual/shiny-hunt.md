A shiny is a Pokémon in rare colours: about 1 in 8,192 in these games. Hunting one can take thousands of encounters, and keeping count by hand is half the work. PokeDaisy counts for you. It reads the game as you play, so the count moves by itself.

It only counts. It never changes your game or your save.

## Starting a hunt

Open the `HUNT` tab under `MORE` and tap `START A HUNT`, then pick how you're hunting:

- `ENCOUNTERS`: every wild Pokémon you meet counts, in grass, caves, on the water or with a rod. A double battle counts two.
- `RESETS`: for a starter, a gift or a legendary you load a state for again and again. Each new Pokémon after a `LOAD STATE` (or `RESTART GAME`) counts once the companion has read it: the one in the battle, or the one that joins your party. If a load gives you the very same Pokémon as last time, it doesn't count, and a line on the game screen tells you to wait a moment longer before you walk up or press the button. Some games replay the same result from a state unless you change your timing.
- `EGGS`: every egg that hatches in your party counts.
- `BY HAND`: nothing counts by itself, only the buttons.

Start a hunt during a battle and its wild Pokémon becomes the hunt's target. Hunts are kept per game, and one hunt is going at a time. While one is going the `HUNT` tab joins the tab bar if there's room. Add it in `SETTINGS`, `TAB BAR` to keep it there.

## The HUNT tab

Everything you need while hunting is on one screen, nothing to scroll:

- On the left, the Pokémon you're after as it looks shiny (from your game, in the games that keep shiny colours), with its name. Tap it to pick another target: one from the battle you're in, the wild Pokémon of the place you're in, or `ANY SHINY`.
- On the right, the count in big numbers with your odds under it, and `+1` and `-1` beside it.
- Under the count, your numbers: `BY NOW` (the chance of having met a shiny in this many tries; going "over odds" is normal, at 8,192 tries it's only about 63%), the next of 50%, 75%, 90% and 99% and the count it comes at, `TIME` (play time while the hunt was going, not counting pauses), `PER HOUR`, and `PHASES` once you've met a shiny of another Pokémon on the way. A phase doesn't end the hunt: the count goes on.
- A `RESETS` hunt has two more buttons, so you can reset from the companion without reaching for a hotkey: `LOAD SLOT` loads the save state slot you're on (the one the `STATES` tab has picked), and `RESTART GAME` starts the game again from its save, asking first. In the DS games a fresh start is usually what gives you a new Pokémon.
- `FOUND IT` ends the hunt, marked found at its count. `OPTIONS` opens the rest.

`OPTIONS` has:

- `TARGET` and `METHOD` (above).
- `ODDS`: the game's own (1 in 8,192, or what `SHINY ODDS` is set to), or one you pick: for a charm that raises the odds, a breeding method, or a ROM hack's own odds.
- `COUNT`: add or take away many at once, for a hunt you'd counted somewhere else before.
- The `PHASES`, each with the count it came at.
- `PUT ON HOLD` (the hunt keeps its count for later) and `NEW HUNT`.
- `EARLIER HUNTS`: this game's other hunts, found or on hold. Tap one to continue it or delete it.

## When a shiny shows up

When the hunt meets its shiny it ends by itself, marked found at that count. Fast-forward and turbo stop, the game's level-up tune plays and a line on the game screen names the Pokémon and the count.

For a few seconds after, and for as long as its battle lasts, `LOAD STATE` and `RESTART GAME` wait: pressing them only shows a line saying a shiny is out. A slip of the load button can't throw it away. Once the battle is over, or the shiny you got from a gift or an egg has been on screen for a few seconds, they work again.

Shinies show up by themselves in the games where `SHINY ALERT` works: the GBA games, the Game Boy Color games and the DS games. In other games a hunt still keeps its count and time, and you count with `+1`. The Game Boy's first games have no shinies, so they have no `HUNT` tab.

## On the game screen

With one screen, the `HUD` shows the hunt's count beside the balls, with the target's picture and the chance so far. Tap it to open the companion, or on a `BY HAND` hunt to count one. Turn it off in `HUD`, `SHINY HUNT`.

Your hunts go with your other settings when you save a [settings file](install.md#move-your-settings-to-another-device).
