The companion knows your whole game: every creature in the Pokédex, every move a Pokémon will learn, every boss ahead. That's handy on your tenth run and a shame on your first. With `SPOILERS` on `HIDE`, the companion only shows what the game itself has shown you, and anything past that is "???".

## Turning it on

The first time you play a new save, a card asks whether you'd like to play without spoilers: on the companion's screen with two screens, over the game with one. `NO SPOILERS` hides, `SHOW EVERYTHING` doesn't. If you let the card go by, nothing changes and it asks again next time, while the save is still new.

A save that's further along is asked once, the first time you open the `DEX` or the `GUIDE`.

You can change it any time in the companion's `SETTINGS`, `COMPANION`, `SPOILERS`. It's kept per game, so you can play one game blind and keep everything in another.

## What it hides

- **The Pokédex.** A Pokémon you haven't seen is "-----" with no picture, as in the game's own Pokédex. Its page stays shut, with `SHOW IT` if you'd like to look anyway. The `NAME` filter only offers letters of Pokémon you've seen.
- **Evolutions.** In the `DEX`'s `EVOLVE` and the `GUIDE`'s `EVOLUTIONS`, a Pokémon you haven't seen is "???".
- **Moves still to come.** In the `DEX`'s `MOVES`, the moves a Pokémon learns past the level you have it at are "???". In a summary's `LEARN`, the next levels' moves are "A NEW MOVE". Tap one to see it.
- **Bosses.** `NEXT BOSS` shows the next one only, and says how many come after.
- **What's around.** The wild Pokémon on `HERE` and on the `HUD`'s `WILD` stay "???" until you've seen them (a tap still shows one). A gift or a trade of a Pokémon you haven't seen doesn't name it until you tap it.
- **Battles.** A trainer's Pokémon still in their balls can't be opened. Of a foe you see its types and what it's weak to, but not its ability, held item, nature, stats or moves.
- **Flying.** `FREE FLIGHT` and the scenic `FLY` only go where you've been, whatever `FLIGHT AREA` says.

What counts as seen is your game's own Pokédex, plus every Pokémon the companion has read in your party, your boxes and your battles. Whatever you tap open stays open in that game.

## What it doesn't hide

Your own things stay: your party's numbers, your bag, your boxes, the map (the game's own map names every place). Matchups stay too, so the battle's hints still work. Helpers like the PC from anywhere keep their own switches.
