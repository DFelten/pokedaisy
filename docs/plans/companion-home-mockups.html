HOME, every companion size

Wireframes for the planned HOME tab, every screen drawn at the same scale: one GBA pixel is the same size in all of them, so you can compare what really fits. Colours and pictures are stand-ins; the real tab uses the companion's own windows, the game's party art and its region map.

What the wireframes assume

  • HOME is the house chip at the start of the tab bar, outside the five picked tabs; the companion opens on it.
  • The tab bar is 48 GBA px tall, 32 on a short companion (under 316 GBA px).
  • Small cards follow the player's order. A card that doesn't fit is left out on that screen, never cut off. Nothing scrolls.
  • The Retroid Pocket Duo is drawn as the Duo Lite's 1280x960 until its bottom screen is measured.
